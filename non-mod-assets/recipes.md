# Recipes
This document contain a list of all recipes in the mod. It's a reference for both players and developers, as the recipes here are added
before they are implemented in the game. The recipes are subject to change, and may not be final.

This document also includes a list of all dish items along with information about their effects, as well as a list of all ingredients and their properties
if they don't come from the base game. This is to help players and developers understand the mod's content and how to use it effectively.

You can treat this document as part of the mod documentation.

## Terms used in this document
- **Workstation type**: The type of workstation used to craft the recipe. It can take following values:
    - **Stove**: Workstation added by the mod for cooking boiled and fried meals. It's also the default option for recipes that doesn't match a more specific workstation.
    - **Oven**: Workstation added by the mod for cooking baked meals.
- **Nutrition**: The amount of hunger points restored by the meal.
- **Saturation**: The amount of saturation points restored by the meal.
